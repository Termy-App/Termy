# Termy App

**A mobile app designed for fast and flexible academic schedule management.**

*This official repository is used to store general information about the app and track issues.* \
*It does NOT contain any source code of Termy.*

If you have a feature request or bug report, please [create issue](https://github.com/Termy-App/Termy/issues) or contact us via [email](mailto:Termy.support@gmail.com).

## Automatic event generation
Building your timetable doesn't require tediously editing events week after week — just configure a few parameters, and the events are generated for you.

Regular calendar apps just aren't built for planning out a long-term schedule. Class schedules tend to have irregular recurrence patterns, plus one-off changes, cancellations, and reschedules — and together, that makes time management slow and exhausting.\
**Termy was built to make this process simple:**

- **Smart planning system** - set up your weekly timetable once, and the app generates your schedule for the weeks ahead.
- **Simple one-off changes** — move a class, cancel it, or shift it to another day — without breaking the rest of the schedule.
- **Lightning-fast data filling** — autocomplete for subjects, instructors, durations, and more.
- **Import/Export** a whole term's schedule as a single file.

## Stay on top of your schedule
Built natively for iOS with close attention to details, the app feels smooth and responsive, with fluid animations and a thoughtfully crafted interface.
Get **real-time updates** at a glance with widgets and Live Activity.
